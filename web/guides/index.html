Guides to a better job application

Short, practical and opinionated. Each guide explains a method you can follow by hand, and then shows the part CareerPilot does for you.

How to tailor your résumé to a job description

The six-step method: pull the terms that matter, map them to what you did, rewrite the summary, skills and bullets, keep every fact. About 7 minutes.

How to write a cover letter that matches the job

Four paragraphs, under 350 words, no "I am writing to apply". Proof instead of adjectives, and why the letter must agree with the résumé. About 6 minutes.

How to answer job application questions

"Why this company", STAR stories, salary, availability and authorisation questions, and keeping every answer consistent with your documents. About 6 minutes.

How to track your job applications

A pipeline with the right stages, what to record, when to follow up, and the weekly review that tells you what is working. About 5 minutes.

Why these four

Almost every application comes down to the same four pieces of work: a résumé that speaks the posting's language, a cover letter that agrees with it, honest answers to the screening questions, and a record of what happened so the next application is better than the last. CareerPilot was built around exactly these pieces; see how it works and the feature list.
