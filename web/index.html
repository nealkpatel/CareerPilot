CareerPilot is a free, self-hosted web app for job seekers and the teams that apply on their behalf. It tailors each résumé, cover letter and application answer to the posting with AI, runs on your own DeepSeek account with no API key, scrapes job links, keeps a job pool from public boards, and tracks every application through screening, interviews and offers.
