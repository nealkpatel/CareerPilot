From a job link to a tailored application in seven steps

Most of the work happens once: you connect your AI account and describe yourself. After that, each application is a paste, a check and a click, and CareerPilot keeps track of the rest.

  1. Create your account

    Sign up at the front page. The first account on a new installation becomes the platform master; later sign-ups are approved by the master. Teams can also have their accounts created for them by their admin.

  2. Connect your DeepSeek account, once

    Open Settings → My DeepSeek account and click Connect DeepSeek. A normal Chrome window opens on the computer that runs CareerPilot and is streamed live into the app: you sign in there with your DeepSeek email or phone number, solve any check the site shows, and click Done. Nothing is attached to that browser, so DeepSeek sees an ordinary sign-in. CareerPilot then confirms the sign-in worked and keeps it in a browser profile that only your requests use. There is no API key to buy or paste.

  3. Describe yourself in a profile

    A profile holds the facts the AI must keep: name and contact details, each employer with dates and notes about what you did there, education, certifications and background notes. You can keep several profiles (for example one per target role) and set a default prompt and template for each. The more concrete the notes, the better the tailoring.

  4. Paste a job link

    Click New application and paste the posting's URL. CareerPilot reads the posting (Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby through their public data; other sites through their structured data, page text, or a full browser render) and fills in the title, company, location and description. Warnings appear if you already applied to that company or link, or if the posting trips one of your keyword rules.

  5. Tailor the documents

    Click Save & tailor documents (or press Ctrl+Enter). The request joins your personal queue and runs in a hidden browser on your DeepSeek account. The prompt combines your instructions, the profile facts, the posting and a strict output format. The reply is parsed into a structured résumé and a cover letter, checked for completeness, and regenerated in a fresh chat up to three times if something is missing. Application answers follow automatically.

  6. Review, edit, export

    Read the result in a live preview. Change the summary, skills, bullets, titles, cover letter or answers in the editor; each save is a new version. Switch between twelve ATS-friendly templates or adjust the style, then download the PDF or Word file, copy the text, or publish a 30-day share link to the résumé.

  7. Apply, then track

    Submit the application on the employer's site yourself and move it along the pipeline: Saved → Ready → Applied → Screening → Interview → Offer (or Rejected, Withdrawn). The dashboard turns this into response rates, a funnel and a 30-day activity chart, so you can see what is working.

What happens behind the scenes

Each person has their own engine and their own DeepSeek session. One person's requests run one at a time; different people's run side by side. The browser that does the work stays hidden, closes after ten idle minutes and reopens on the next request. The profile controls names, contact details, employers, dates and education; the model writes the summary, skills, bullets, cover letter and answers. Everything is stored in a database on the computer that runs CareerPilot.

Want to look around first? The platform master can switch the AI engine to Demo mode, which produces placeholder documents instantly so you can explore the templates, editor and pipeline before connecting an account.

How long does it take?

Connecting DeepSeek and writing a thorough profile takes about fifteen minutes the first time. After that, importing a posting takes seconds, and a tailored résumé, cover letter and set of answers typically arrive in one to three minutes depending on DeepSeek's speed. Reviewing and exporting is as quick as you want it to be, and you never retype the same facts twice.
