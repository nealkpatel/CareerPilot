Everything CareerPilot does for a job application

CareerPilot is an AI job application assistant: it turns one profile and one job posting into a tailored résumé, cover letter and application answers, exports them in ATS-friendly formats, and tracks the application until there is an answer. Here is the complete list of what it does.

Tailored documents for every job

You keep one profile with the facts that must never change: name and contact details, employers, dates, education, certifications and notes about each role. For every job, the AI writes what should change: the summary, the skills list, the bullet points under each role, the cover letter and the answers to application questions. Facts come from you; wording comes from the model.

  • Résumé tailoring that mirrors the posting's language and priorities without inventing experience.
  • Cover letters written from the same tailored résumé, so they agree with it.
  • Application answers for screening questions, produced automatically after generation or on demand.
  • Quality checks: every reply is validated (well-formed, the right number of roles, enough depth in the bullets) and regenerated in a fresh chat up to three times if it falls short.
  • An editor for the summary, skills, bullets, role titles, cover letter and answers; edits are saved as a new version without calling the AI again.

Paste a link, get the posting

Add an application by pasting the job's URL. Postings on Greenhouse, Lever and Ashby are read through their public data; other pages are read through their structured JobPosting data, then the page text, then a full browser render for pages that only work with JavaScript. The title, company, location and description land in the form for you to check before tailoring.

A job pool that fills itself

The job pool imports postings from Remote OK, Remotive, Jobicy, Himalayas and We Work Remotely, plus any Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby board you add. Search it, filter by how recently a job was posted or whether it is remote, save or hide postings, mark them applied, and start a tailored résumé with one click.

Templates built to pass applicant tracking systems

Twelve single-column, ATS-friendly presets, researched from the layouts software engineers use most: Jake's Classic, ATS Classic, Modern Minimal, Harvard Serif, Compact Engineer, Stanford, Accent Line, Developer Mono, Executive Serif, Clean Slate, Classy and Gradient Edge. A style editor with live preview covers typography, layout, colours, contact block, experience, education, certifications, headline, expertise, projects, section styles and PDF decorations. Styles are saved per user, templates can be switched without regenerating, and your own HTML templates can be uploaded.

PDF, Word and text

Download any version as PDF or Word, or copy the plain text. Word files follow the same fonts, colours and section order.

Live preview and history

See the document as you work; every generation and edit is kept as a version you can go back to.

Share links

Publish a résumé at a private link that expires after 30 days, can be revoked at any time, and counts views. The cover letter and application data are never shared.

Prompt library

Your own prompts with placeholders, a separate prompt for answering questions, and a preview of the full prompt before it is sent. The output rules are always appended for you.

Track every application to the offer

Applications move through a clear pipeline: Saved → Ready → Applied → Screening → Interview → Offer, or Rejected and Withdrawn, each with a timeline of status changes. The dashboard shows applications, applied, interviews, offers and response rate, a 30-day activity chart, a pipeline funnel and your recent work. Switch between a table and a board view, filter by status or profile, and search.

Warnings catch mistakes before they cost time: duplicate companies or job links are flagged, and keyword rules you define (security clearance, citizenship, on-site or hybrid, travel…) can warn, ask for confirmation, or block an application.

Your own AI account, your own data

CareerPilot does not sell AI credits and needs no API key. Each person connects their own DeepSeek account once, from any computer: the sign-in window opens on the computer running CareerPilot and is shown live inside the app, so you sign in there like in any browser. Your requests then run with your account, one at a time in your own queue, side by side with everyone else's. CareerPilot itself keeps all data in a database on the computer you run it on.

It works for one person as well as for teams that apply on behalf of candidates, has light and dark themes, works on phones, and is free to run. See how it works step by step, or read the frequently asked questions.
