Built for teams that apply on behalf of candidates

Career coaches, reverse-recruiting services, staffing agencies and bootcamp career teams submit dozens of applications a day for other people. CareerPilot gives each candidate a profile, each team member their own AI account, and the whole team one pipeline.

Three roles, clear boundaries

RoleWhoWhat they do
MasterThe platform ownerApproves new accounts, creates admins, moves bidders between teams, resets passwords, sets job sources and the import schedule, warning rules and sign-up mode, and reads the audit log. Sees who has connected an AI account, never the account details. Does not bid.
AdminA team lead or coachOwns the candidate profiles, prompts and templates. Creates bidder accounts, assigns profiles to them, and sees their bidders' applications and documents. Can tailor and apply too.
BidderA team member who appliesWorks with the profiles assigned to them: imports postings, tailors documents, submits applications and moves them along the pipeline, with their own AI account and their own queue.

A day in a bidding team

  1. The admin prepares a candidate

    One profile per candidate: contact details, every role with dates and notes on what they actually did, education and certifications. The admin picks a default prompt and template for that candidate, so every bidder produces documents in the same voice and layout.

  2. Bidders pick jobs

    From the shared job pool (Remote OK, Remotive, Jobicy, Himalayas, We Work Remotely and any Greenhouse, Lever or Ashby boards the master adds) or from links they find. Duplicate-company and duplicate-link warnings stop two bidders from applying to the same place twice; keyword rules block postings the candidate cannot take (clearance, citizenship, on-site, travel).

  3. Documents are tailored and checked

    Each bidder's requests run on their own DeepSeek account, in their own queue, so a busy colleague never slows anyone down. Every reply is validated and regenerated when incomplete. The bidder reviews, edits if needed, downloads the PDF or Word file and submits.

  4. Everyone sees the pipeline

    Status changes (Applied, Screening, Interview, Offer…) and their timeline are visible to the bidder and their admin; the dashboard shows response rates per team. The master's platform overview shows applications and AI résumés across all teams, who has connected an account, and the audit log of who did what.

Safeguards that matter when you apply for others

  • Facts stay fixed. The model can only rewrite the summary, skills, bullets, cover letter and answers; employers, dates, titles and education come from the profile the admin maintains.
  • Separate AI accounts. Each person signs in to their own DeepSeek account; the sign-in lives in a browser profile only their requests use, and nobody else can see or use it.
  • Approval-based sign-up. New accounts wait for the master, or sign-up can be closed entirely so only invited people get in.
  • Share links without the rest. A public résumé link never exposes the cover letter, the answers or the application history, expires after 30 days and can be revoked.
  • Your own server. CareerPilot runs on a computer you control, with its database on that computer, and can be published to the web through Netlify with the server staying at home.

What it is not

CareerPilot does not submit applications by itself and does not hide that a team is involved. It prepares honest, tailored documents quickly and keeps the team organised; the submission, and the judgement about which jobs fit the candidate, stay with people.
